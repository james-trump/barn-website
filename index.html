Still very much a working farm

The barn sits on a ten-acre smallholding just outside the village of Coton, four miles from Whitchurch. It was a working farm building long before it became somewhere to stay, and the conversion has kept that history close: reclaimed brick, oak beams overhead, and stone underfoot through the ground floor.

Inside, the open-plan kitchen and living space is built around a wood burner, with doors opening straight onto the patio and garden. It suits a big family gathering just as well as a quiet week away — there's room to spread out, and enough character that nobody mistakes it for a hotel.

Where you'll sleep

Three bedrooms between them sleep seven, and each has its own character.

Bedroom one · sleeps 2

The Master Bedroom

Tucked into the eaves with a large skylight and doors onto a juliet balcony. A super-king bed that can be made up as twin singles on request, and reclaimed oak furniture.

Bedroom two · sleeps 2

The Round Window Room

Named for the porthole window that looks straight out over the neighbouring farmer's field, framed by the branches of a tree just outside. Dressed with a king-size bed, with a heavy oak wardrobe and calm, restful walls.

Bedroom three · sleeps 3

The Family Room

A double bed plus a separate single bed, so it makes for a brilliant family room. French doors let in plenty of light, with a view out over the garden. The Family room sits on the ground floor with a bathroom just nextdoor so it's great if you struggle with stairs.

  • Three bathrooms in total — one with a bath, all with showers — so there's rarely a queue in the morning.
  • A full kitchen with an induction hob, electric oven, dishwasher and fridge-freezer.
  • A round table that seats six, with the garden and fields in view while you eat - you may even spot our flock of Hebridean sheep.
  • Dogs welcome, up to two by arrangement, with an enclosed garden to let them off the lead.

Ten acres, a hot tub, and not much else to hear

The garden is fully enclosed, so children and dogs can run around off the lead. Beyond the lawn, the smallholding opens out into paddocks and hedgerows, with a bench at the far end that catches the evening sun. The hot tub is wood-fired rather than electric — it takes a little longer to heat, but costs the countryside nothing to run.

Coton, near Whitchurch

The barn sits in the quiet village of Coton in north Shropshire, four miles from Whitchurch and its shops, pubs and Tuesday market. It's good walking and cycling country — Offa's Dyke Path and the Whixall Moss nature reserve are both close by, and the Llangollen branch of the Shropshire Union Canal is about three miles away.
